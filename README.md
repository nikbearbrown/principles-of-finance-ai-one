# Principles of Finance (AI Plus One)

## About the book

This rough draft (version 0.1) is a first course in business finance for readers comfortable with percentages, basic algebra and charts. Follow a fictional print cooperative from statements and dated cash flows to valuation, risk and funding decisions. You will calculate a defensible result and explain which assumptions could change it. Accounting helps but is not required for the refresher; no coding or model account is necessary.

## How to teach or study it

Begin with decisions and statements before valuing future dollars. Then read historical risk without treating it as a promise, and apply what you know to operating choices. Work the dates and assumptions before consulting a tool. The [decision brief](assessment/decision-brief.md) asks you to reason across a bill, a bond and a business order. These rates and cases are fictional; course adoption and expert review have not occurred.
