# Principles of Finance (AI Plus One)

**Original course rough 0.1.** Twenty core chapters plus an optional checking chapter. Not an expert-edited replacement for an existing book or an adopted course.

This is a first course in business finance, for readers comfortable with percentages, basic algebra and simple charts. Prior introductory accounting helps but is not required for the refresher in Chapters 4–5. No statistics, coding or AI account is required. A calculator and a sheet of paper are enough. Work the dates and assumptions first; optional critique comes afterward.

The goal is to choose a measure that matches a decision, do a calculation someone can reproduce and explain what would change the choice. A fictional print cooperative connects cash, financing and risk decisions. A separate fictional household teaches saving and investing. The material does not advise any actual investment or replace professional judgment.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this draft, readers should be able to:

- Interpret financial statements and compare cash flows that occur at different dates.
- Use valuation and risk measures to compare projects, securities, and financing choices.
- Make and independently check a recommendation for a fictional household or firm.
