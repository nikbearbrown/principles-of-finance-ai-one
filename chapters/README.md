# Principles of Finance (AI Plus One): chapter guide

[Return to the book overview](../README.md).

### Part 1 · Read the decision and the firm

1. [Find the Finance Decision](../chapters/01-find-the-finance-decision.md): Identify a choice, its feasible alternatives and the financial evidence needed.
2. [Ownership and Control Are Different](../chapters/02-ownership-and-control.md): Separate ownership claims from who controls particular decisions.
3. [Name the Rate Before Multiplying](../chapters/03-name-the-rate.md): Match a rate and its time basis to the amount you are measuring.
4. [Record Now, Collect Later](../chapters/04-record-now-pay-later.md): Distinguish recognition from collection on a dated cash ledger.
5. [Link the Statements Without a Plug](../chapters/05-link-the-statements.md): Reconcile statements without inserting a balancing plug.
6. [Read Ratios Without Turning Them Into Causes](../chapters/06-read-ratios-in-context.md): Read liquidity and profitability ratios in context without inferring causes.

### Part 2 · Put dated dollars and securities on one basis

7. [One Payment, Two Dates](../chapters/07-one-payment-two-dates.md): Convert one payment between two dates.
8. [A Payment Schedule Has a First Date](../chapters/08-price-equal-payments.md): Value equal payments and identify the first payment date.
9. [Price the Schedule, Not the Total](../chapters/09-price-uneven-payments.md): Value irregular payments one by one on a shared basis.
10. [A Bond's Coupon Is Not Its Yield](../chapters/10-price-bond-cash-flows.md): Price a coupon bond and distinguish coupon from yield.
11. [Find What the Owner Actually Gets](../chapters/11-value-the-equity-claim.md): Identify what the equity claim receives and how it is valued.

### Part 3 · Read uncertainty and choose exposure

12. [Read a Market History Without Treating It as a Promise](../chapters/12-read-return-history.md): Explain what a return history can and cannot say about the future.
13. [Average Return Is Not Ending Wealth](../chapters/13-separate-average-and-dispersion.md): Contrast average returns, dispersion and ending wealth.
14. [A Slope Is Not a Guarantee](../chapters/14-interrogate-a-regression.md): Interpret a fitted slope without treating it as a guarantee.
15. [Match the Portfolio to the Question](../chapters/15-match-the-portfolio-to-the-question.md): Match the portfolio and its measure of risk to the question.

### Part 4 · Put the decision into practice

16. [Choose by Value, Not by Percentage Alone](../chapters/16-choose-by-value-not-percentage.md): Compare projects by incremental value instead of ranking percentages alone.
17. [Trace the Money From New Claims](../chapters/17-trace-the-money-from-new-claims.md): Trace how issuing new claims changes cash and ownership.
18. [A Sales Forecast Is Not a Cash Forecast](../chapters/18-build-the-cash-forecast.md): Convert sale assumptions into a dated forecast of receipts and payments.
19. [What Does a Supplier Charge for Twenty More Days?](../chapters/19-price-a-suppliers-extra-days.md): Compute the cost of extra supplier credit days.
20. [Limit the Exposure You Mean](../chapters/20-limit-the-exposure-you-mean.md): State which exposure is being limited before choosing the hedge.
